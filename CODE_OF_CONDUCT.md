# Code of Conduct

BentoDesk welcomes contributors in Chinese or English.

Be direct and respectful. Discuss the work, not the person. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not accepted in issues, discussions, reviews, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when needed to keep the project usable. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content; contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub profile when public discussion would expose personal information.
